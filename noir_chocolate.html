The Science of Pleasure

Dark chocolate is
one of the world's
most powerful foods.

  • Dark chocolate with 70%+ cacao is packed with flavonoids — powerful antioxidants that neutralize free radicals, outperforming blueberries, acai, and red wine by up to 40 times per gram.
  • Regular consumption of high-cacao dark chocolate has been clinically associated with reduced LDL ("bad") cholesterol, improved blood flow, and lower blood pressure — protecting your heart over time.
  • Cacao stimulates the production of serotonin and phenylethylamine in the brain — natural mood-elevating compounds that reduce anxiety and create a sustained sense of wellbeing.
  • A 40g serving of NOIR contains more magnesium than a cup of spinach, along with significant levels of iron, zinc, and potassium — essential minerals for energy metabolism and immune health.
  • The flavanols in dark chocolate improve cerebral blood flow, supporting sharper memory, focus, and long-term cognitive resilience. Studies link regular intake to reduced risk of cognitive decline.
  • Unlike milk chocolate, ultra-dark cacao has a very low glycemic index — it doesn't spike blood sugar and can even improve insulin sensitivity when consumed in modest daily amounts.

From Soil to Soul

The ancient
groves of
Ecuador.

At 1,200 metres above sea level, in the mist-wrapped valleys of the Napo province, grow the rarest cacao trees on earth. Some are over 200 years old. Their beans carry the volcanic minerality of the Andes and the humid sweetness of the Amazon basin.

We work directly with 34 farming families. No brokers. No commodity markets. Each pod is harvested by hand, fermented on-site for seven days, and sun-dried on raised beds until the moisture content reaches exactly 6.5%.

The result is a chocolate with a flavour complexity no other origin can replicate — deep tobacco, leather, dried cherry, and the faintest whisper of earth after rain.
